About Us

A local team you can trust with your next move

Pearl Heights Properties was built to make buying, renting, selling, and managing property in Kampala and Wakiso straightforward — no jargon, no vanishing agents, no surprise fees.

Our Story

Started with one simple frustration

Pearl Heights started because finding a decent, honestly priced home in Kampala shouldn't take weeks of dead-end viewings and vanishing agents. We built a small, focused team that actually inspects every property before it's listed, and answers WhatsApp messages the same day.

Today we help clients buy, rent, sell, and manage residential property across Kampala and Wakiso — from studio apartments in Muyenga to family villas in Kololo.
